# Flower Escape gameplay demo

Open `Scenes/FlowerEscape_UI_Demo.unity` and press Play. Drag a shop card onto an empty leaf cell to buy a gear. Drag a placed gear to an empty cell to move it, onto another kind to swap, or onto the same kind and level to merge. Dropping outside the board keeps the gear in its old cell. **Refresh** costs 5 coins and rotates the cards; the yellow 50-coin button adds demo currency.

Press **START!** to fight three waves. The two characters on the boat fire visible projectiles at the nearest advancing enemy; equipped gear count controls fire rate and total gear level controls damage. Enemies have health bars, move and shoot back. Hits show damage numbers, kills award coins, and the boat can lose health. The four boosters heal the boat, stun enemies, damage all enemies, or fire a stronger beam. The yellow X returns to Preparation. A clear or defeat can be replayed by returning and pressing START again.

The scene is generated by **Tools > Flower Escape > Build UI Demo**. Rebuilding saves the scene and the `GearCell` and `ShopCard` prefabs. It does not modify the source art or SampleScene.

## Responsive layout

Canvas Scaler uses **Scale With Screen Size**, reference **480 × 1120**, Match **Width**. The `SafeAreaRoot` follows `Screen.safeArea`; background extends to the full screen. The controller switches to a compact layout below a height/width ratio of 1.7. The same 6 × 4 logical grid remains in both states, with square cells sized from available board width and height. Combat moves and shrinks the board, expands the battle area and places boosters beside the board.
